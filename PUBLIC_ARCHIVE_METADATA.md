## Description

This archive accompanies the manuscript “Separator algebras and transfer theory for the zero-forcing polynomial.” It contains the manuscript source and PDF, exact finite certificates, independent certificate verifiers, deterministic certificate generation, explicit tree/block/cactus algorithms, arbitrary-separator audits, pinned Python dependencies, expected outputs, build/reproducibility instructions, and SHA-256 integrity metadata. The arbitrary-q separator and connection-rank results are proved symbolically in the manuscript; finite assertions such as r_2=60 and the 32-state cycle response quotient are independently machine-verifiable from the included certificates.
